HOW TO PLAY

  • Pick 1 to 5 fingers, or the thumb (6), at the same time as Dhruba.
  • Batting: different numbers = you score your number. Same number = OUT!
  • Bowling: match Dhruba's number to get him out.
  • An innings ends when all wickets fall. The chaser must beat the target.
  • Dhruba learns your habits, so mix it up!
